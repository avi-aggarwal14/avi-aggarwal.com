While I'm in lessons, I've got AI agents doing my work.

For context, I run hypjam, a UGC agency, and I'm still in school. So I can't do everything myself.

## What they actually do

One works through the to-do list on my calendar.

One researches a company before I get on a call with them, so I go in knowing what they sell, who they sell to and what they've already tried.

And one rebuilt my personal website (this one) while I told it what to change.

## What I do

I mostly just check their work.

The trick is writing instructions like you're briefing a really fast intern: what you want, what done looks like, and what it shouldn't touch.

Then you spend your time on the stuff only you can do.

Like filming the reel for this.
